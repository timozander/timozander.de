> Originally published as a conference talk for [WeAreDevelopers World Congress 2022](https://www.wearedevelopers.com/en/videos/in-the-dawn-of-the-ai-understanding-and-implementing-ai-generated-images).

This session focused on AI-generated images and the ideas behind them: how generative models can translate prompts into visual output, where the underlying techniques come from, and what it takes to move from the theory to an actual implementation.

It also looked at the practical side of the topic — the trade-offs, the terminology, and the gap between the impressive public results and the engineering work required to make such systems understandable and usable.
